Refund & Exchange Policy

We want you to be confident buying from Golden Chair King. Here's exactly how exchanges, warranty and refunds work.

7-day easy exchange

  • Unused chairs in original condition and packaging can be exchanged within 7 days of delivery.
  • Change of mind: you bear the transport cost for sending the chair back.
  • Damaged or wrong item: we cover transport and make it right — report within 24 hours of delivery.

Warranty claims

  • Selected models carry up to a 1-year replacement warranty on structure and manufacturing.
  • Normal wear, misuse, water/heat damage and self-repair are not covered.
  • Start a claim on WhatsApp with your order reference and clear photos.

Refunds for advance payments

  • If we cancel an order (out of stock, etc.), advance payments are returned via bKash/Nagad within 3–5 working days.

Not eligible

  • Custom-made, custom-colour or bulk corporate orders are not returnable unless defective.

How to start

  • WhatsApp +880 1521-431725 with your order details — we'll guide you through in minutes.
